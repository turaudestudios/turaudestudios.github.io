Stormmorph

Be the tornado! Eat the town, mutate into fire, water or lightning, and grow huge. A two-minute tornado brawl on a low-poly island town.

THE GAME

START TINY. END HUGE.

You start as a tiny twister. Suck up bins and cones, then benches, cars and houses, and finally whole buildings. Every bite makes you bigger, and buildings rip apart chunk by chunk while you keep moving. Steer with one finger: drag anywhere to move.

WHY IT'S FUN
01

What you eat changes your storm

Hydrants and water towers make a Waterspout with a huge pull. Fuel cans and gas stations make a Fire Tornado that eats one size up. Power plants unleash a Lightning Storm.

02

Eat or be eaten

Rival tornadoes are hungry too. Eat anything smaller, run from anything bigger. In the last 30 seconds the Storm Surge hits: points double and the leader wears a crown.

03

Play with friends

Jump into a Quick Match, or create a private room and share a 6-letter code. Pick 2 to 10 tornadoes, the map and the power-ups. Bots fill every empty slot.

04

Towns to wreck

Day, Sunset, Night and Snow, festive Halloween and Holidays towns, and two sweet ones: Candy Land and the Chocolate Factory. Every round can look different.
