GAME FACT SHEET

STORMMORPH.

Be the tornado! Eat the town, mutate into fire, water or lightning, grow huge.

Stormmorph is a two-minute tornado brawl on a low-poly island town. You start as a tiny twister and eat your way up from bins and cones to whole buildings. What you eat changes your storm: water towers make a Waterspout, gas stations a Fire Tornado, power plants a Lightning Storm. Rival tornadoes are hungry too, and in the last 30 seconds the Storm Surge doubles the points and crowns the leader. Play solo against bots, jump into a Quick Match, or invite friends to a private room with a 6-letter code.
