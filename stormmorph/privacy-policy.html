Stormmorph Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Stormmorph (Android package com.turaudestudios.stormmorph) handles. Stormmorph is made by Turaude Studios.

What the game itself stores

Your progress (coins, upgrades, skins, best score and settings) is saved on your device. Stormmorph does not ask for your email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Uninstalling the game deletes the data on your device.

Online features

Stormmorph uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Leaderboards, Lobby, Relay, Cloud Code and Cloud Save) for its weekly leaderboard, online rooms and player names. You never see a sign-in screen; the game signs in by itself:

  • Anonymous player ID. Unity creates an anonymous player ID for your copy of the game the first time an online feature is used: when you finish a standard round (your score is posted to the weekly leaderboard), open the leaderboard, or tap ONLINE. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Weekly leaderboard. After each standard round your best score of the week and your player name are stored on Unity's servers and shown to other players on the worldwide leaderboard.
  • Player name. The name you type (or an automatic one such as "Player1234") is shown to other players in rooms and on the leaderboard. Names are unique, so the game keeps a list of taken names on Unity's servers. Names are filtered for offensive words. There is no chat.
  • Online rooms. In a room, your name, the skin your tornado wears and your gameplay in the match (your tornado's position, size and score) are shared with the other players in the room and passed through Unity's servers.
  • IP address. Unity's servers see your device's IP address while you are connected, so the game can reach them and the other players.
  • Google Play Games (when available). If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game links your anonymous player ID to your Play Games account and keeps a copy of your progress (coins, upgrades, skins, best score and settings) on Unity's servers, so it follows you to a new phone. We receive your Play Games player ID only, never your email address or Google password.

Unity acts as our service provider under its own privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Deleting your online data: open Settings → DELETE ONLINE DATA in the game. This deletes your player ID, your name (it becomes free for others) and any saved copy of your progress from Unity's servers straight away. A score already on the weekly leaderboard stays until that week's board resets (at most 7 days); email us (below) if you want it removed sooner, or to have us delete everything for you.

Purchases

Optional purchases (Remove Ads and cosmetic skins) are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to payments: policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

Stormmorph shows ads through Unity LevelPlay mediation, with ads from networks such as Unity Ads and Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, these providers may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. They act under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (LevelPlay, Unity Ads): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Players who buy Remove Ads see no interstitial or banner ads; optional rewarded ads stay available only when you choose to watch one.

Children

Stormmorph is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data security

Information sent to the online, advertising and payment providers above is encrypted in transit.
